    <p>
        We propose a hybrid deep learning model for drought detection that integrates climatic indices (multi‑scale SPEI, precipitation, temperature) with data‑driven approaches, using a dual‑branch RNN with convolutional lateral connections. Experiments show it outperforms both standalone indices and purely data‑driven models, demonstrating the potential of hybrid systems that combine domain knowledge with learning.
