        <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1569843225002109" target="_blank">Calibration and uncertainty quantification for deep learning-based drought detection</a>
    </p>
    <p>
        We introduce calibration and entropy‑based uncertainty quantification for deep learning‑based drought detection, addressing miscalibration and overconfidence. Our method reduces expected calibration error to 0.31% and improves uncertainty‑based decision‑making accuracy from 72.27% to 76.59% by rejecting uncertain samples. Empirical results across European drought events demonstrate enhanced reliability and trustworthiness for climate decision‑making.
